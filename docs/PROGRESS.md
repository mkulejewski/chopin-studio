# Practice progress and musical objectives

Progress separates personal repertoire levels, current machine evidence, recall history and user-defined technical work.

- **Piece time** unions identified, non-discarded Practice activity intervals and saved Learning session intervals per source session. It includes pauses within an activity, but does not double-count overlapping intervals. Unidentified work is not assigned to a piece.
- **Measure progress** uses the latest analysis matching each activity's current piece, boundaries, revision, completeness and discard state. Reanalysing one take does not create another attempt. Histories remain separate per digital reference and a fingerprint of its notes, edition and quality. Editing a reference does not merge incompatible histories. Ambiguous repeated-theme locations are excluded from measure and recurring-mistake totals. Counts show aligned groups and potential missing/extra notes, not measure mastery. Located time assigns successive score-anchor intervals up to eight seconds to the preceding measure; longer pauses and unlocated intervals remain unattributed. It is an estimate rather than an exhaustive time ledger.
- **Recurring differences** require the same measure/kind/pitch in at least two distinct current activities. Repeated notes within one activity count once. Reference, alignment and performance intent may explain a discrepancy; recurrence does not certify a mistake.
- **Memorization retention** shows evaluated, unassisted hidden/review Learning tests, separated by lesson revision, passage, hand and approved note-rule fingerprint. Manual recollections remain separately labelled and do not become automatic pitch passes. Ordinary visible-score practice does not demonstrate memory. Pass counts, most recent result, days since the last test and longest successful retest gap are descriptive evidence. The seven-day review reminder is a simple rule, not a predicted probability of forgetting or a spaced-review scheduler. Historical lesson revisions remain labelled.
- **Technical objectives** have their own title, skill, optional measure range and completion flag. They do not change personal mastery or Learning stages. Add and edit them in the piece's Progress details.
- **Problem time** is explicitly attributed from a recorded activity interval to an objective. Overlap counts once per objective/session; the same time may be associated with several problems, so problem totals must not be summed as exclusive piece time. Attribution is retained independently of candidate segment IDs when a timeline is rebuilt or undone, and its contribution is clipped to current matching, non-discarded activities. You can remove an attribution in the objective details. Objective edits use revision checks to prevent overwriting another edit.

Storage uses migration 006. Existing recordings, analyses, personal mastery and Learning state are preserved. New passage evidence requires reanalysis; no historical data is invented from older aggregate-only analyses. These summaries are offline and make no cloud requests.
